# ADR 0015: Header shield without the University wordmark

Date: 2026-10-13

Status: Accepted

## Decision

Show the University of Waterloo shield and the letters UW at the top right of the hosted app and
the static preview. The text beside that mark says “Independent website made by students.” Do not
use the University wordmark, and do not present ClearCourse as an official University website.

The earlier full wordmark linked to the University site. This decision keeps the shield the owner
asked for and replaces the institutional name with the independent-student notice.
